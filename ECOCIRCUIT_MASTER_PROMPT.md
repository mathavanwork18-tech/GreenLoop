# EcoCircuit — Master UI/UX + Product Build Prompt

## 1. PROJECT IDENTITY

**App Name:** EcoCircuit  
**Tagline:** "Reuse Electronics. Reduce E-Waste."  
**Core Positioning:** EcoCircuit is an AI-enhanced e-waste marketplace where users can sell unwanted electronics, sell individual components/spare parts, find reusable devices, buy used components, exchange or donate electronics, and send unusable hardware for certified recycling.

---

## 2. TECHNOLOGY

- Flutter & Dart
- Material 3 Design System
- Responsive layout (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop)
- Clean architecture & reusable components
- Multi-theme support (Dark Mode + Light Mode + System Default)
- Local mock state & dummy data persistence

---

## 3. CORE PILLARS & FEATURES

1. **Light & Dark Mode Theme Engine**: Full Material 3 `ColorScheme` with emerald green accents, high contrast, and persistent theme switcher.
2. **Onboarding Experience**: 3-step carousel introducing reuse, parts harvesting, and recycling.
3. **Authentication**: Login, Registration with location selector, password strength indicator, and remember me.
4. **Home Dashboard**:
   - Dynamic Header with EcoPoints balance & notification bell
   - Prominent search bar with voice UI ready
   - Hero banner: *"Turn Old Electronics Into New Possibilities"*
   - Quick Action buttons: Sell, Find Parts, Donate, Recycle, Exchange
   - 15+ Electronics & Component Categories
   - Nearby Products with live distance indicators (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Bengaluru, etc.)
   - Featured & Recommended feeds
   - Interactive Eco Impact counter & "How It Works" 4-step guide
   - Social proof, testimonials & responsive desktop footer
5. **Electronic Parts Breakdown**: Unique feature showing complete device vs individual harvestable components (RAM, SSD, Display, Battery, Motherboard, Charger) with independent pricing.
6. **Smart Compatibility Checker**: Select device model + target component (e.g. Dell Inspiron + 8GB DDR4 RAM) → Instant Compatibility evaluation.
7. **8-Step Sell & Post Flow**:
   - Category selection
   - Device details & specifications
   - Smart condition assessment questionnaire (Power, Display, Battery, Physical damage)
   - Multiple image simulator
   - Fair price estimation
   - Location tagger
   - Review & QR Code Product ID generator (`EC-2026-XXXXX`)
8. **Marketplace & Advanced Filters**: Multi-criteria filters by category, condition, price slider, brand, location distance, and sorting options.
9. **Negotiation Engine (Make an Offer)**: Submit custom offer amount with instant status updates.
10. **In-App Seller Messaging**: Real-time mock conversation bubbles with attached product card.
11. **Cart, Checkout & Order Timeline**: Multi-step checkout with delivery address, UPI / Card / COD payment options, and visual 6-stage order tracker.
12. **Donations & Exchange Hub**: Dedicated modes to donate electronics to schools/NGOs or swap devices with nearby users.
13. **Certified Recycling Requests**: Request doorstep pickup for hazardous e-waste & view TNPCB-partnered recycling centers.
14. **Sustainability & Eco Impact Dashboard**: Live counter of kg e-waste diverted, CO₂ avoided, and EcoPoints level progression (*Beginner → Recycler → Eco Champion → Sustainability Hero*).
15. **Product Comparison Matrix**: Side-by-side spec and condition comparison tool.
16. **Settings & Trust Center**: Appearance switcher, notification controls, privacy toggles, battery safety guides, and expandable FAQs.
